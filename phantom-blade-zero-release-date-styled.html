Phantom Blade Zero Release Date: When Does It Come Out?

Phantom Blade Zero is out now. Its worldwide release date was October 29, 2026 for PC and PS5. In US Pacific time, launch access began on October 28 at 7:00 p.m. PDT, explaining Steam's October 28 date. It is available now. (official site and Steam, checked 2026-11-09).

You can play on Windows PC through Steam or Epic Games Store, or on PS5, including PS5 Pro enhancements. No PS4, Xbox, or Nintendo Switch edition is listed as of November 9, 2026. US digital prices are $59.99 Standard and $69.99 Deluxe; Deluxe adds two outfits, a digital artbook, and a soundtrack. (PlayStation Store, Steam, and Epic Games Store, checked 2026-11-09).

The PC version requires 145 GB of SSD storage. Metacritic lists PC at 88/100 from 52 reviews and PS5 at 86/100 from 48 reviews. The reported 134.857 GB PS5 size comes from secondary store tracking. Multiplayer cross-play does not apply to this single-player game, and PC-to-PS5 cross-save is unconfirmed. (Steam, Metacritic, and secondary PS5 size report, checked 2026-11-09).

What each edition includes

There are two digital editions, with the same base game content. The Deluxe Edition adds cosmetics and digital extras, not a confirmed story expansion. PlayStation official edition comparison.

  • Standard Edition ($59.99 US) — full base game only; pre-ordering also offered early unlocks of the Treasure Basin accessory and Legacy outfit.
  • Digital Deluxe Edition ($69.99 US) — base game plus Wulin Legend outfit, Blood-Steel Form outfit, Digital Artbook, and Digital Soundtrack (OST). There is no announced story DLC or season pass included in this bundle.
  • Pre-order bonuses (not a third edition) — Treasure Basin accessory (10% increased Remnant Dust drop rate) and Legacy outfit, both available through in-game progression without the early unlock. Sony and the publisher list these as pre-order incentives, so do not assume new post-launch purchases still receive the early unlock. PlayStation bonus details.
  • Digital Deluxe Upgrade ($10.00 US on Steam) — for owners of the Standard Edition who want the Deluxe extras; this is not a story expansion. Steam add-on listing.

For players who want only the game, the standard version contains the full story. The $10 Deluxe premium buys two outfits and two digital media extras.
